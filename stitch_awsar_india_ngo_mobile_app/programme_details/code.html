About the Initiative

AWSAR India works directly at the grassroots level in urban settlements and vulnerable areas to safeguard children from abuse, abandonment, and exploitation. Through active synergy with National Child Helpline 1098 and government stakeholders, our rapid response and field workers provide immediate rescue, psychosocial aid, and institutional rehabilitation.
